Enhancing the Teaching of Mechanical Engineering Craft Practices in Technical Colleges for Sustainable Self-Employment in Kogi State

Abstract

This study was set to determine the strategies for enhancing the teaching of mechanical engineering craft practices in technical colleges for sustainable self-employment in Kogi State. The population comprised 80 teachers and 54 instructors in technical colleges in Kogi State. Findings revealed that mechanical craft teachers and instructors need practical skills improvement for teaching drilling and milling practices.
