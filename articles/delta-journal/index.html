Impact of Psychological Counselling on the Economic Survival of Families in Isi-Uzo LGA of Enugu State: A 21st Century Approach

Published: July 1, 2026
Volume: 1, Issue: 5
Pages: 832-847

Abstract

This research focused on the impact of psychological counselling for economic survival in the family: A 21st century approach. The study adopted a quasi-experimental research design with 224 families in Isi-Uzo Local Government Area in Enugu State. The results revealed a significant impact of psychological counselling on the economic survival of the family.
