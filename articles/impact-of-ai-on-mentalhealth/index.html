Abstract

The study examined the impact of artificial intelligence on the mental health of postgraduate students in Abia State, Nigeria. A sample of 200 postgraduate students was selected using a multi-stage sampling technique. Findings revealed that AI-assisted academic activities, AI dependency, and frequency of AI use have a high extent of impact on the mental health of postgraduate students in Abia State. The study concluded that artificial intelligence has both beneficial and potentially adverse effects on the mental health of postgraduate students, depending on how it is used.
