Abstract

The study assessed the effectiveness of online vendor platforms in promoting micro-business performance of Technology and Vocational Education (TVE) students in universities in Ebonyi State. The result revealed that online vendor platforms are highly effective in promoting revenue growth and meeting high market reach of micro-businesses of TVE students in universities in Ebonyi State.
